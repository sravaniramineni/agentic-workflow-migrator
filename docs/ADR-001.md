# ADR-001: Deterministic planner with LLM-assisted extraction

## Context
Migration recommendations must be explainable to business stakeholders.

## Decision
Keep step classification deterministic and scored (risk/confidence); use LLMs only for
extraction and summarization, never as the final authority on financial/legal steps.

## Consequences
- Recommendations are auditable and reproducible.
- LLM upgrades cannot silently change risk decisions.
